Pete’s Gmail Task Importer

A personal tool that turns selected Gmail messages into actionable tasks in Toodledo.

Privacy policySupport

From an email to a task

  1. The account owner labels an email .IncompTask in Gmail.
  2. The importer reads selected messages and creates tasks in the owner’s connected Toodledo account, including a link to the original email.
  3. After a successful import, it removes the processing label to help prevent repeat imports.

Personal use

Maintained by Pete Macke for his own authorized accounts. This app is not available for public signup. Google authorization is handled through Google’s consent flow.

Why Gmail access is needed

Selected email content provides task titles and notes. Label access identifies messages selected for processing and updates their label after import.

This public website provides app information only. It does not run the importer or collect Gmail credentials or messages.

Independent personal project; not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Toodledo.
